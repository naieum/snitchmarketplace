# Repairing a beat chain

Read when revising an episodic outline or when connector labels look correct but the events
do not affect one another. These are original teaching examples, not finished timed scripts.

## A sequence with no engine

A delivery robot wants to leave a parcel. It sees a fountain. It dances with a pigeon.
A door opens. It delivers the parcel.

Replacing the sentence openings with BUT and THEREFORE changes no relationship. The
delivery would work if the fountain and pigeon were removed. The ending requires no choice.

## The same ingredients made consequential

| Beat | Action and result | Connection |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | A delivery robot arrives with a parcel at a home beside a fountain. A sign says “Ring bell for delivery”; the bell is above its reach. A pigeon watches from the gate. | Establish the delivery goal, obstacle, and usable surroundings. |
| B2 | The robot stands on the parcel to reach the bell. Its weight crushes the box, leaving it too low again. | THEREFORE: the unreachable bell motivates using the only available step; the tactic creates damage and fails. |
| B3 | The robot spots the pigeon on the high gate and performs a fluttering dance, pointing from the bird to the bell. The pigeon pecks the bell. | THEREFORE: the failed step motivates recruiting something that can reach; the dance becomes a tactic. |
| B4 | The door opens, but the pigeon claims the crushed parcel as a nest and pecks the robot's reaching claw. | BUT: the delivery is finally possible, yet the helper now prevents handing over the parcel. |
| B5 | The robot scoops water from the fountain in its cupped claw and sets it beside the parcel. The pigeon leaves the box to drink. | THEREFORE: losing access motivates a distraction using the established fountain. |
| B6 | The robot gives the damaged parcel to the waiting resident. It unfolds into a flat-pack birdhouse; the resident hangs it at the gate, and the pigeon moves in. | THEREFORE: the distraction enables delivery, which reveals what the earlier nesting behavior set up. The goal resolves despite the cost of the first tactic. |

The parcel's contents create a final reframe, not a new power that saves an unsolvable
situation. The resident opens only after the bell rings. The fountain is within reach
before it becomes useful. The dance has a result; it is not a detachable intermission.

The example still needs performance choices, visual staging, and believable time allocations
before it becomes a 2–5 minute script. A causal outline alone does not prove it is funny,
clear in motion, or the correct duration.

## Repair patterns

| Symptom | Check | Useful repair |
|---|---|---|
| “THEREFORE a meteor falls” after a character orders soup | Did ordering soup cause or motivate anything about the meteor? | Establish a mechanism, use the meteor as a relevant external complication, or replace it. |
| “BUT a dog walks past” | Does the dog obstruct an attempt or change what the character can do? | Make the dog take a needed prop, reveal information, or remove the beat. |
| Every attempt fails the same way | What has changed beyond the costume or joke? | Carry damage, lost time, discovery, or commitment into the next attempt. |
| A clever solution appears only in the final scene | Could the viewer know this capability existed? | Plant the rule or prop earlier, or solve through an established capability. |
| A funny detour can move anywhere | Does its result affect a later choice? | Let its gag create the obstacle, supply the resource, or expose the belief that drives the next beat. |
| A reaction shot is flagged as “AND THEN” | Is it a new story beat or the audience reading the existing result? | Keep a purposeful reaction within the beat and budget its screen time. |
| The dialogue explains a missing physical action | Can a storyboard show the cause without the explanation? | Stage the action and preserve the dialogue only if it adds character or new information. |

Do not force every pause or shot into this table. Test changes in the story's situation;
use the smaller units to make those changes legible and enjoyable.
