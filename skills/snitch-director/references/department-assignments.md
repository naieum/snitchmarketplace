# Optional department assignments

Read when the user asks for specialist agents or a team workflow. These are bounded roles
that can be performed locally or assigned through the host's actual delegation mechanism.
They are not installed agents or permission to call services. Focused cinematography,
production design, storyboard, editorial, and sound Skills exist; assigning those roles
does not invoke the Skills automatically. When the user requests a focused deliverable,
call the Skill tool with "snitch-cinematography", "snitch-productiondesign",
"snitch-storyboard", "snitch-editor", or "snitch-sound" as applicable, one Skill per
call. Do not spawn a team
simply because several departments are mentioned in a film brief.

The director owns the integrated plan. Establish story version, scene intent, constraints,
asset IDs, geography, and protected decisions first. Then independent proposals can proceed
in parallel if requested and supported. Coverage depends on blocking; continuity and editorial
checks need a candidate sequence. Parallelizing those dependencies before inputs exist
creates incompatible plans rather than useful independent work.

| Role | Bounded input | Deliverable | May not silently change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cinematography | Selected scenes, staged layout, visual rules, equipment/medium limits | Shot viewpoints, movement, focus and light proposals with reasons and feasibility questions | Blocking, script, asset identity, duration target |
| Production design | Script/scene inventory, geography needs, look and production constraints | Character/location/prop descriptions, palette/materials, asset gaps and state requirements | Story facts or cast/location limits |
| Storyboard / layout | Staged scene and candidate shot cards | Panel descriptions, poses, subject/camera paths, unreadable transitions | Protected reveals or actions; propose changes explicitly |
| Editorial | Candidate sequence, source inventory, intended durations and speech/action timings | Selected cut logic, missing coverage, pacing, pickups, and revised timing with assumptions | Protected dialogue, ending, supplied performance rate |
| Sound | Candidate sequence, script, acoustic geography, dialogue and cue constraints | Sound spotting, source position, cue sheet and recording/design needs | Protected dialogue, ending, picture decisions |
| Continuity checker | Candidate plan plus canonical input/asset records | Findings with shot/scene evidence; checked transitions and Skips | The candidate plan itself; returns critique first |

Assignment brief:

> Work as [role] on [exact scenes/shots] using [input version and supplied artifacts].
> Preserve [constraints and protected decisions]. Return [specific deliverable], naming shot
> IDs and assumptions. Propose changes outside your scope rather than applying them. Do not
> generate media, contact anyone, or write over other departments' files. Stop when this
> bounded deliverable is complete.

Each role reads its actual owning Skill or the relevant references in this Skill through the
host's available loading mechanism. Do not invent a named specialist skill that is not
installed. Give an independent
checker the actual input and candidate plan, not the desired verdict.

## Integration and stopping

Reconcile by scene intent, then protected constraints, then readability/continuity, then
production economy. An unexplained disagreement is not solved by taking a vote. Record the
chosen option and downstream effects. If a choice changes the approved story, present that
change explicitly rather than hiding it as a camera fix.

One proposal pass and one targeted correction pass is a useful default. Further passes need
a specific unresolved defect, not a wish for a higher vague quality score. Keep remaining
uncertainty visible. Record which roles actually ran, which were done locally, and what
material was unavailable. Separate cost/time estimates from measured work.
