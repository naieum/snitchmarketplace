# ads.txt — Authorized Digital Sellers (IAB Tech Lab)
# https://iabtechlab.com/ads-txt/
#
# Purpose: tells SSPs/exchanges which sellers are allowed to monetize this domain.
# Without ads.txt, programmatic demand drops 20-40% (buy-side filters traffic from unverified sellers).
#
# Format per line:
#   <domain of seller>, <publisher account ID>, <DIRECT|RESELLER>, <TAG-ID>
#   - <domain>: the SSP's canonical domain.
#   - <pub ID>: your account/seller ID at that SSP.
#   - DIRECT: you have a direct contractual relationship with the seller.
#   - RESELLER: a third party (network) is authorized to sell on your behalf.
#   - <TAG-ID>: optional 40-char hex from the SSP's certification authority.
#
# Comment lines start with `#` — keep yours so future maintainers know why each line exists.
#
