## Section 302 -- Financial Reporting Controls

**Mapped Snitch Categories:** 37 (Logging and Monitoring), 60 (Audit Logging)

### Control Description

Corporate officers must personally certify that financial reports are accurate and complete. Technical controls must ensure the integrity of systems that generate, process, and store financial data. Audit trails must be maintained for all financially significant transactions.
