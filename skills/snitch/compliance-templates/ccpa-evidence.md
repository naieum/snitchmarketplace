## Right to Delete

**Mapped Snitch Categories:** 28 (Authorization & Access Control (IDOR)), 53 (CCPA & SOX Compliance)

### Control Description

A consumer shall have the right to request that a business delete any personal information about the consumer which the business has collected from the consumer. The business must verify data deletion endpoints exist and function correctly.

## Right to Opt-Out

**Mapped Snitch Categories:** 53 (CCPA & SOX Compliance)

### Control Description

A consumer shall have the right, at any time, to direct a business that sells or shares personal information about the consumer to third parties not to sell or share the consumer's personal information.
